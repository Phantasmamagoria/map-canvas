Map Canvas

Concept maps that turn into Anki cards.

Map Canvas is a personal study app for the Mac and an Android phone. You break what you read into a map of ideas, and the app turns the parts you mark into Anki image-occlusion cards.

Your maps are kept as plain files on each device. If you connect Google Drive, the app also keeps them in a folder of your Drive called Map Canvas, which is how your devices stay in step.

It's made for one person's own use and isn't offered to the public.
