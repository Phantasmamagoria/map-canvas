Privacy policy

Map Canvas · updated 25 October 2026

What the app can see in your Google Drive

When you connect Google Drive, Map Canvas asks for Google's drive.file permission. That lets it create files and use the files it created, in a folder called Map Canvas in your Drive. It can't see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

What it keeps

Your maps, earlier versions of them, and pictures you add to them: as files on your own devices, and, once you connect Google Drive, in that Map Canvas folder.

What it shares

Nothing. Map Canvas has no server of its own: each device talks only to Google Drive, directly. There are no analytics, ads or tracking, and no one else receives your data.

Removing access

You can remove Map Canvas's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the Map Canvas folder from your Drive.

Google API Services

Map Canvas's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
